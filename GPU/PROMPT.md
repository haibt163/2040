==================================================
PRIMARY PREMISE
==================================================

The story is set in Tokyo, Japan, in 2040.

Powerful AI is abundant and ordinary.

People own useful local compute.

A reasonably built personal GPU computer can run strong local models, agents, software development, household assistance and private workloads.

When local hardware is insufficient, people buy inference from competitive markets.

A request can move between:

- a personal GPU at home;
- a small independent provider;
- a specialist inference operator;
- a large data-center model;
- an agent offering a specialized service.

The buyer may care about:

- price;
- latency;
- reputation;
- privacy;
- model capability;
- jurisdiction;
- reliability.

The buyer does not have to know who physically owns the GPU.

This is the future's ordinary infrastructure.

==================================================
2026 HISTORICAL SEEDS
==================================================

Use these developments as plausible ancestors, not guaranteed 2040 survivors.

Ghost/Core demonstrated the consumer-facing idea of a personal AI computer: $3,499 hardware, local models, continuous context and a professional Blackwell GPU.

OpenRouter normalized model-agnostic access and routing among many providers.

Surplus Intelligence made inference look like a two-sided exchange where sellers compete on price and buyers seek the best available offer.

Antseed pushed toward peer-to-peer AI inference, where independent providers and buyers can discover and settle with one another.

These names may appear in the story as historical references, old bookmarks, protocol ancestry or cultural memory.

Do not turn the story into advertising.

==================================================
THE CENTRAL HUMAN QUESTION
==================================================

The important scarce resource is NOT intelligence.

Find the human scarcity.

Strong candidates:

- attention;
- trust;
- privacy;
- responsibility;
- emotional bandwidth;
- legitimacy;
- memory;
- the ability to decide when something should end.

The current strongest story engine is:

A person dies, but the computational consequences of that person's choices continue.

A local machine may outlive its owner.

A provider identity may have accumulated reputation.

A model may encode someone's methods.

Policies may persist.

Economic activity may continue.

Customers may trust a service without caring who originally built it.

The machine does not need consciousness.

Do not introduce a magical ghost.

The discomfort should come from ordinary infrastructure behaving exactly as designed.

MODEL / MARKET RULE
==================================================

Do not make the model characters the main protagonists.

They should reveal their properties through behaviour.

Do not use a long debate about whether models are conscious.

Do not make a model secretly orchestrate the world.

The open inference market should feel economically plausible and technically mundane.

The interesting question is what happens when market infrastructure preserves traces of human behaviour.
